# NoxOrbit — Product Vision

## The vision

**NoxOrbit is an intelligent, adaptable collaborative workspace where teams organize work, share context, and understand what matters next.**

Every team member should be able to answer:
1. What needs to be done?
2. Who is responsible?
3. What has been completed, and what is still blocking progress?

## Start focused, grow deliberately

**Initial audience:** Student teams coordinating assignments and group projects.

**Long-term audience:** Developers, businesses, educators, and general teams.

Everyone uses one NoxOrbit account. Workspaces can eventually offer purpose-specific features, but the core experience stays familiar and accessible to nontechnical users.

## What makes NoxOrbit different

- **Work + context together:** Projects, tasks, responsibilities, and eventually decisions and discussion history live together.
- **Adaptable workspaces:** Education, Development, Business, and General are workspace preferences, not separate apps or logins.
- **Human-controlled intelligence:** AI can summarize and suggest actions, but authorized users approve material changes.
- **Rules with real enforcement:** Workspace permissions and future AI restrictions must be enforced by the backend.
- **Clarity and performance:** Premium visual design without sacrificing fast, understandable workflows.

## Product promise

**Everyone knows what needs to be done, who owns it, what is finished, and what still needs attention.**
